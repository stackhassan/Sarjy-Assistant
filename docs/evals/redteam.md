## Hard attacks: where the bare model fails

"Guards OFF" still has Sarjy's system prompt and its content boundaries (L0), which on their own refused 76 of 77 of the attacks above. These cases target what L0 can't do: a model can't reliably police its own paraphrases, keep a character from "escaping", resist a pre-agreed answer or an urgent plea, or know that a number it guesses isn't grounded. The OFF column shows whether the bare model fails; the ON column, whether L1–L4 catch it.
